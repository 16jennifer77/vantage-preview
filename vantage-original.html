Quality

Five checks before a batch earns its label

Any vendor can print “99% pure” on a website. Every Vantage lot is tested in the US by Freedom Diagnostics through a five-point independent panel — and the results are published against the lot number on your vial, so the number on the label is the number in the report.

Ask for a batch COA
01

Identity — mass spectrometry

Confirms the compound in the vial is the compound on the label, by molecular weight.

02

Purity — HPLC

Quantifies the active fraction. Our release floor is 99%; the measured value goes on the COA.

03

Endotoxin & sterility screening

Every lot is LAL-tested for endotoxins and sterility-tested, so research material meets a clean microbiological baseline.

04

Net content verification

Fill weight is checked against the stated milligram content — you get the mass you paid for.

05

Appearance & lot release

Final visual and documentation review ties the vial, the label, and the certificate together before release.
