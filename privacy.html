Privacy Policy

This policy covers the data this portal collects and how it is handled. It applies to account holders and visitors alike.

What we collect

Account information: the email address, name, and password credentials you use to create an account (passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us).

Order information: shipping and billing addresses, phone number if provided, and your order history — items, quantities, prices, payment method, and any payment reference you report.

Submissions: messages you send through the contact form and restock-notification requests ("notify me" sign-ups), which store your email address and the product you asked about.

How we use it

We use this data to fulfill orders, communicate with you about your orders and payments, respond to your messages, and notify you when a product you asked about is restocked. We do not use your data for advertising profiles.

Where it is stored

Account, order, and submission data is stored in Supabase (hosted Postgres and authentication). Order confirmation and notification emails are sent through our email delivery provider. Session and authentication cookies are set on your browser to keep you signed in; we do not run advertising or analytics trackers.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers required to operate the store (database hosting, authentication, and email delivery), each limited to the data needed to perform its function.

Your choices

You may request a copy, correction, or deletion of your account and submission data at any time by emailing us. Note that deleting order records may limit our ability to resolve payment or shipping disputes for those orders.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: info@vantagebiology.com.
